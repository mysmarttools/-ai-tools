Meta Title Best Practices


  • ✅ Optimal Title Length: Keep your title between 50–60 characters (approx. 580 pixels) to avoid clipping on SERPs.
  • ✅ Keyword Placement: Place your primary target keyword closer to the beginning of the title tag.
  • ✅ Brand Inclusion: Add your brand name at the end separated by a pipe (|) or hyphen (-).
  • ✅ Unique Snippets: Ensure every page on your website has a unique, descriptive meta title.
  • ✅ Avoid Keyword Stuffing: Focus on clickability and clear communication rather than repeating words.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Meta Title?

A meta title (or title tag) is an HTML element that specifies the title of a web page. It is displayed on search engine results pages (SERPs) as the clickable headline for a given result.

What is the recommended Meta Title length?

The recommended title tag length for desktop and mobile search engine optimization is between 50 and 60 characters.

How does a Meta Title impact SEO?

Title tags are a primary direct ranking factor for search engines. Well-crafted titles also increase Click-Through Rates (CTR), driving more organic traffic to your web pages.
