Text Image Design Tips


  • ✅ Contrast Focus: Choose high-contrast text and background colors to ensure legibility across all screens.
  • ✅ Font Hierarchy: Use heavy bold fonts like Impact or Arial for headers, and clean serif fonts for formal quotes.
  • ✅ Gradients & Shadows: Enable text shadows or outlines when overlaying typography on complex background images.
  • ✅ Optimal Canvas Resolution: High-definition default canvas ensures graphics look crisp when posted on social media.
