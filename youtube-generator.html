YouTube SEO Best Practices


  • ✅ Target High Intent Keywords: Include your main keyword within the first 3–5 words of your video title.
  • ✅ First 2 Lines Matter: Search engines and viewers see the first 150 characters of your description before the "Show More" fold.
  • ✅ Use Timestamps & Chapters: Break long videos into structured sections to rank for specific search queries on Google.
  • ✅ Strategic Hashtags: Include 3–5 relevant hashtags (#YouTubeSEO, #ContentCreator) at the bottom of your description.
