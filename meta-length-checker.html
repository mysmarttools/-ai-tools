Meta Tag Length Best Practices


  • ✅ Meta Title Limits: Keep titles under 60 characters (or ~580 pixels) to prevent truncation in search engine results pages (SERPs).
  • ✅ Meta Description Limits: Aim for 150–160 characters (or ~990 pixels) on desktop, and around 120 characters for mobile views.
  • ✅ Primary Keyword: Place your main target keyword as close to the beginning of the title as naturally possible.
  • ✅ Avoid Truncation: Exceeding limits will cause Google to display an ellipsis (...) which can decrease your Click-Through Rate (CTR).

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Meta Title and Description length important?

If your titles or descriptions are too long, search engines like Google truncates them. Keeping them within recommended limits ensures searchers see your full value proposition.

Does pixel width matter more than character count?

Yes, Google measures snippet titles in pixels (around 580px limit). However, character counts (50-60 for titles, 150-160 for descriptions) provide a reliable real-time estimate.
