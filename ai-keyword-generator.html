SEO Keyword Tips


  • ✅ Target Search Intent: Focus on keywords that solve the user's explicit problem.
  • ✅ Use Long-Tail Keywords: Target 3-4 word phrases for faster ranking and lower competition.
  • ✅ Avoid Keyword Stuffing: Naturally integrate terms in headings, metadata, and body text.
  • ✅ Placement Strategy: Include primary keywords in H1 tags, permalinks, and early paragraphs.
  • ✅ Competitor Research: Analyze competitor keyword density to spot untapped opportunities.
